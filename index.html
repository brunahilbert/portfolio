about me

Art teacher turned
frontend developer —
based in Stuttgart.

I'm a Brazilian web developer living in Germany, and my path into tech was anything but conventional. Before writing a single line of code, I spent years as a Visual Arts teacher — and that experience shaped everything about how I build for the web.


I think about beauty and function as inseparable. A great interface isn't just one that works — it's one that delights. I bring that perspective to every project I build.


After completing my Full-Stack Web Development course at CareerFoundry, I've been building with React, Angular, TypeScript, and Node.js — always looking for that sweet spot where aesthetics meets engineering.
